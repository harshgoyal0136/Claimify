CLAIMSHIELD - OUR APPROACH IN PLAIN WORDS
=========================================
Hackathon: Adrosonic Build, 24 h. Problem Statement 2: AI-Powered Synthetic Identity &
Deepfake Claim Detection. Source of truth for decisions: docs/FINAL_APPROACH.txt.
Detail: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md. Step-by-step commands: docs/RUNBOOK.md.


1. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING
-----------------------
An insurance claim comes with evidence: damage photos, invoices / medical bills (PDF or
scans), and an ID card + selfie. ClaimShield checks every piece and answers, for an
insurance adjuster:
  - Is anything fake, AI-generated or edited?
  - WHERE (boxes on the photo, changed lines in the PDF)?
  - WHY (one plain sentence per check)?
  - How sure (a score 0-1 and a band: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / UNCERTAIN)?

Pitch: "Every other team ships a detector. We ship a detector AND the fraudster."
A built-in red-team "arena" makes new fakes from real photos and shows we still catch
them - including a generator family (Flux) the detector never saw in training.


2. WHY THIS APPROACH (the key decisions)
----------------------------------------
a) Several independent signals instead of one big model.
   One detector is easy to fool and can't explain itself. Signals that look at
   different things (camera metadata, pixel statistics, learned features,
   reconstruction error) fail in different ways, so together they are robust, and
   each one gives the adjuster its own reason.

b) Win by subtracting.
   Six things that work beat twelve half-working ones. Cut on purpose: video,
   multi-agent setups, lighting/shadow physics, hyperbolic geometry, a GAN gate.
   New ideas go to docs/IDEAS.md, not into the code.

c) Deterministic scoring - the LLM never decides a number.
   The final score is a fixed weighted formula (configs/weights.yaml). Same input ->
   same score, every time. Judges and insurers can audit it. The LLM only rewrites the
   explanation text, and only if it answers within 4 s; otherwise the instant template
   text stays.

d) Fast first, deep later (two tiers).
   What a judge feels is the first result on screen. Fast checks must show the first
   card in <= 2 s and the full fast score in <= 8 s on a normal laptop CPU (no GPU at
   demo time). The slow check (AE reconstruction) runs in the background and never
   blocks the screen.

e) "Generalizes to unseen fraud" must be a NUMBER, not a claim.
   Flux images are held out: never used for training or calibration. We report how well
   we catch them. That is the honest proof of generalization.

f) Abstaining is allowed.
   Tiny / blurry / heavily compressed image -> band UNCERTAIN instead of a guess,
   unless there is hard evidence (C2PA credentials, a PDF version diff).
   "Not applicable" (e.g. PNG has no JPEG tables) is shown as a grey card, not a fail.

g) Swappable parts for deployability.
   The forgery localizer (TruFor) has a non-commercial licence, so it sits behind an
   interface with a fallback we built ourselves. The demo must run on the fallback once.

h) Privacy.
   No personal data goes to the LLM (placeholders). The audit log stores file hashes,
   not files. Uploads are deleted after scoring.


3. HOW IT WORKS (the pipeline)
------------------------------
Upload -> each file is routed to one of three lanes -> claim-level checks -> score ->
explanation -> reviewer UI (Streamlit) -> audit log (SQLite).

IMAGE LANE (damage photos, scans, selfies)
  Fast tier (CPU, seconds):
    - Forensics: C2PA content credentials (e.g. "made by Adobe Firefly"), EXIF camera
      data, JPEG quantization tables, copy-move detection, noise residual.
    - CLIP probe: frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 features + a small trained classifier
      (real vs fake).
    - Localizer: heatmap of WHERE the edit is (TruFor, or our fallback).
    - Family map: which generator family it looks like (SD1.5 / SDXL / Firefly / ...),
      or "unknown family" - shown as a tree.
    - Regions: boxes on the photo with a reason per box.
  Deep tier (background):
    - AE reconstruction (AEROBLADE idea): pass the image through a Stable Diffusion
      autoencoder; AI images rebuild with suspiciously low error. Always on, supporting
      weight 0.20, confidence capped at 0.7.

DOCUMENT LANE (invoices, medical bills, PDFs, scans)
    - PDF structure: hidden earlier versions (incremental updates) -> "time machine"
      diff of what was changed; producer/creator mismatch; odd fonts on digits.
    - OCR with per-word confidence (pasted digits often read worse).
    - Field checks by document type: items add up, tax rate valid, dates in order,
      invoice number / ICD-10 format.

IDENTITY LANE (ID card + selfie)
    - Face match ID <-> selfie (InsightFace).
    - Is the selfie itself synthetic (image fast tier).

CLAIM-LEVEL CHECKS (exactly three)
    1. Invoice dated before the damage photo was taken.
    2. Photos in one claim from different camera models.
    3. Name on the ID differs from the name on the invoice.

SCORE
    Per lane: weighted mean of the signals (weights x confidence).
    overall = 0.5 * max(lanes) + 0.5 * mean(lanes) + claim bonus.
    Bands: LOW < 0.35 <= MEDIUM < 0.65 <= HIGH; UNCERTAIN via the quality gate.
    A waterfall chart shows how much each signal pushed the score.

ARENA (red team)
    Real photo -> fake it live (AWS Bedrock Nova Canvas inpaint -> local SD1.5 inpaint ->
    pre-generated fallback) -> re-score -> show we caught it. Plus the held-out table.


4. HOW WE PROVE IT (evaluation)
-------------------------------
Nothing ships without a number (make eval -> reports/eval_latest.md):
  1. Accuracy (AUC, true-positive rate at 1 % false alarms) on seen generators vs
     held-out Flux.
  2. False-alarm rate on real wild photos at HIGH, with confidence interval (target
     <= 5 %, and no real face at HIGH).
  3. Document tamper precision / recall.
  Supporting: signal x family heatmap (incl. an eval-only GAN row that openly shows the
  AE blind spot), localizer TruFor vs fallback, robustness to compression/WhatsApp,
  latency (make bench -> reports/latency_latest.md).


5. THE DATA AND WHERE IT CAME FROM
----------------------------------
Real (label "real"):
  data/seeds/         1,050 = 1,000 VehiDE car-damage photos (Kaggle, Apache 2.0)
                              + 50 FUNSD scanned forms. Used for training/calib/test.
  data/seeds/whatsapp/ ~100 WhatsApp round-trips of seeds, SAME file names (to collect).
  data/wild/          1,449 = 1,300 COCO val2017 + 149 FUNSD. False-alarm test only,
                              never trains. No overlap with seeds.
  data/wild/faces/    200 LFW real faces (Kaggle mirror).
Fake:
  data/generated/{sd15,sdxl,flux}/{t2i,img2img,inpaint}/  1,800 images (200 each),
      made on the college HPC (BIT Mesra, NVIDIA L40, PBS) with scripts/hpc/generate.py.
      Flux = held-out family.
  Still to make on the laptop: splices + copy-moves, Nova Canvas fakes, tampered
      invoices / medical bills / PDFs, compressed variants (q90/q70/q50/512 px).
  To collect by hand: ~30 Adobe Firefly images (keep C2PA), optional GAN faces.
Known limitation: downloaded seeds mostly lack phone camera EXIF, so the EXIF signal is
weaker in eval; ~20-30 own phone photos are used for the demo cases and bench files.
