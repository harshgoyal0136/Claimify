## Judge Q&A
- **Unseen generator?** Held-out Flux row; AE is training-free; family map puts mass on
  the diffusion node.
- **Why not let the LLM decide?** Explainability is 25 %; non-reproducible isn't
  explainable. The LLM writes prose from evidence, after the score exists.
- **Why not hyperbolic / why not agents / why not video?** Each answered as a decision
  with a reason; they're on the slide.
- **Real-photo false positives?** Real-wild FPR with CI, 200 real faces in the set,
  abstain band, quality gate.
- **TruFor licence?** Swappable interface; fallback rehearsed; deployment uses a
  commercially-licensed localizer.
- **Can this deploy?** CPU-only inference with measured p95, single process, hashes-only
  audit, PII scrubbed, weights in config, Bedrock optional.
